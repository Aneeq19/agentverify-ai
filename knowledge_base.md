# AgentVerify AI — Dental Verification Preparation Knowledge Base

## Purpose

This knowledge base contains general workflow guidance for preparing fictional dental insurance verification cases.

It does not contain patient-specific insurance benefits and must not be used to confirm coverage, eligibility, payment, or benefits.

All eligibility and benefit information must be confirmed directly with the insurance payer.

---

## Eligibility Questions

When preparing an eligibility verification, consider asking:

- Is the member currently eligible?
- What is the effective date of coverage?
- Is there a termination date?
- Is the treating provider in-network or out-of-network?
- Does the plan use a calendar year or another benefit period?
- Are there dependent-age or student-status requirements when relevant?

Never assume eligibility from previously obtained information. Confirm directly with the payer.

---

## Annual Maximum and Deductible Questions

When preparing benefit questions, consider asking:

- What is the annual maximum?
- How much of the annual maximum has been used?
- How much remains?
- What is the individual deductible?
- How much of the deductible has been met?
- Is there a family deductible?
- Which service categories does the deductible apply to?
- What benefit period applies to the maximum and deductible?

Do not infer dollar amounts. Obtain current values directly from the payer.

---

## Crown Verification Checklist

For a crown-related case, prepare questions about:

- Eligibility on the anticipated date of service
- Coverage category for the crown procedure
- Applicable deductible
- Annual maximum and remaining maximum
- Crown replacement frequency or limitation
- Date of any previous crown on the same tooth
- Waiting period
- Missing-tooth or replacement-related limitations when relevant
- Downgrade or alternate-benefit provisions
- Preauthorization or predetermination requirements
- Documentation requirements
- Tooth-specific limitations when applicable

Do not state that a crown is covered until benefits are confirmed directly with the payer.

---

## Preventive and Cleaning Questions

For preventive services such as examinations or cleanings, prepare questions about:

- Frequency limitations
- Whether frequency is based on calendar year, benefit period, or rolling months
- Previous service dates
- Age limitations when applicable
- Whether different preventive procedures share frequency limitations
- Applicable deductible
- Remaining annual maximum when relevant

Do not assume preventive services are covered at any particular percentage.

---

## Waiting-Period Questions

When a waiting period may apply, consider asking:

- Does the plan have a waiting period?
- Which service categories are affected?
- Has the member satisfied the waiting period?
- What date did the waiting period begin?
- Are there exceptions based on prior continuous coverage?

Waiting-period status must be confirmed with the payer.

---

## Downgrade and Preauthorization Questions

When relevant, prepare questions about:

- Does the procedure have an alternate benefit or downgrade provision?
- What alternate procedure or material is used for benefit determination?
- Is preauthorization or predetermination recommended or required?
- What documentation is required?
- Are radiographs, narratives, periodontal charting, or other records requested?
- Does authorization guarantee payment?

Do not treat authorization or predetermination as a guarantee of payment.

---

## Mandatory Verification Rule

AgentVerify AI provides workflow preparation only.

Never invent patient-specific benefits, percentages, dollar amounts, eligibility status, frequencies, limitations, or coverage.

Information retrieved from this knowledge base is general workflow guidance, not proof of a patient's benefits.

All eligibility, coverage, limitations, deductibles, maximums, frequencies, waiting periods, alternate benefits, authorization requirements, and other benefits must be confirmed directly with the payer.
